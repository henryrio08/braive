## Product behavior preserved and clarified

The participant supplies fears, current ability and boundaries at intake. The coordinator plans what unfolds around and to them; the scene supplies sights, sounds, automatic movement and events. It does not require “I swim forward,” voice navigation, command checklists or repeated questions about what to do next. Routine stages have empty or unannounced prompts. Speech appears when it belongs to the situation or provides essential context.

Henry chose to retain the partner's silent-action rule: any needed character dialogue classifies the entire experience as conversation, including mixed walking/conversation situations. This classification still requires physical movement when the task needs it. Speaking windows remain natural, timed and limited to an approved character. New provider schemas enforce empty stage actions and interaction choices; historical command graphs remain readable in saved records.

The partner's automatic progression, completed final stage, latched motion completion, quiet-gap handling, STOP confirmation protection, source character models/palettes and local transcription remain. No voice-to-navigation fallback, topic-specific scene template or preset practice fallback is introduced. One repair remains shared with native validation inside the original 120-second generation deadline.

## Implemented construction subset

- The importer measures normalized rest-geometry bounds for all 355 CC0 GLBs, traversing their original node transforms. Existing manifest metadata, source meshes, rigs, animation clips, textures and palettes are unchanged. All three manifests remain identical.
- Spark adds a functional construction brief. The backend assigns stable task/cast/prop/stage/motion/boundary/outcome requirement IDs. Wonder binds these to actual scene entities, zones, assemblies, anchors, relationships and routes.
- Construction version 1 resolves support/contact, containment, yaw-facing, origin-relative placement and authored parent attachments. It checks references, namespace ambiguity, cycles, usable-zone fit and explicit AABB clearance; three bounded deterministic placement/facing passes are followed by constraint rechecks. It preserves the approved plan, cast, stage IDs, duration and intensity. It does not shrink objects or reparent posed assemblies to evade a failure.
- Anchor points are local, offsets are world-meter deltas. Explicit point targets stay points. Containment needs a node/zone volume. Assembly members must fit their assigned world volume with a 0.2m boundary allowance. Required task/boundary constraints cannot become soft decoration warnings.
- Every path receives a route binding and clearance/eye-height metadata. Anchored world routes resolve entry/end points; viewer floor anchors add eye height. Relative routes retain their captured-heading behavior and native runtime rechecks. Declared route entry positions are checked before execution.
- Seated contact requires an explicit subject contact anchor and target support anchor. Standing bounds never certify a seat. Native support checks consume the contact point; walking away from an initial support is allowed and route checks then govern travel.
- The coordinator describes air/water/mixed medium behavior and names important dynamics the app cannot simulate. Scene medium regions bind water to a real zone and optional water-profile surface, and bind claimed swimming to viewer swim paths. Water visuals cannot masquerade as solid route support. The renderer supplies ripples, bounded fog and foliage light variation; it has no buoyancy, force-driven current, splashing, smoke transport or flowing gas volume simulation. Physical water effects needed for the task remain a declared capability gap.
- Compiler reports are app-owned and excluded from provider output/repair input. New provider output requires construction/environment fields, while saved-record schemas keep them optional.

These are bounded geometric checks, not a physics engine or proof of semantic completeness. Requirement coverage proves explicit references exist; it cannot prove that every arbitrary natural-language boundary is faithfully realized. Bounds are rest AABBs, not exact occupied volumes, semantic fronts or measured posed seat contacts. Authored anchors still need native geometry/pose inspection. Native routes use sampled body/floor probes, not exact continuous capsule collision. Later-stage native review samples stage/end states rather than every animated frame.

## Implemented appearance

`SkyModel.swift` computes a deterministic six-face 128px sky from horizon/zenith/ground/sun colors, light direction, haze, cloud coverage and stored seed. Both eyes share the distant cubemap. Real task geometry must still provide near/middle depth. Enclosed/underwater settings can use a solid background. Explicit fog distances and environment-light intensity are separate controls; stage operations can replace the full environment profile.

`SceneAppearance.swift` supplies a gentle shared luminance-band fragment shader. It preserves geometric facets, hue and alpha, with 3–10 light bands and strength 0–0.35 (defaults 6/0.18). Scene settings can soften or strengthen it. Entire approved character subtrees and unrelated custom fragment programs are protected. Stage material changes retain the selected style.

App-owned matte, ground, water, glass and foliage profiles accept bounded scalars, never model shader code. Ground varies subtly in world space. Water changes normals/color without moving geometry. Foliage changes light without moving silhouettes/collision. Glass uses ordinary transparency and roughness. Water/glass support explicit opacity/sidedness. A shared active-time uniform freezes movement on STOP and resume retains the clock; no extra full-screen effect pass was added.

The sky CPU preview is diagnostic artwork generated from the sampler, not an iPhone screenshot. Device rendering, alpha/depth behavior, shader compilation and sustained performance still need native checks. The current audio inventory is unchanged: this work does not add a water recording, spatial audio or new synthesized sound capability.

Strict-subset mode remains false because the project uses optional fields and union forms; local schema and semantic validation still run. Empty guided action/choice lists also remove deeply nested legacy definitions from new-generation requests. Expanded document nesting is checked against the provider's ten-level limit.
