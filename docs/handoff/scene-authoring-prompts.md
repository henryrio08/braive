# AI-authored guided experiences

The user's fear description, current ability and boundaries are freeform planning data. Turn them into a situation that happens around and to the participant: what they see, hear and experience as it unfolds. Create it from reusable capabilities, never a topic-specific preset. The participant experiences the situation; they do not navigate or direct a scene by speaking. Do not require first-person action statements, WALK, SIT, ASK or NEXT commands, or recurring “what do you want to do?” questions.

Generation should usually finish in a few minutes for an ordinary description. Produce a complete, valid plan and scene in one focused pass: choose the smallest coherent cast and setting that realize the actual activity, boundaries, automatic stages and usable routes. Favor registered assets and reusable operations over many bespoke parts; keep descriptions and repeated fields concise. Add geometry, effects and speech only when they improve the experience or satisfy a concrete spatial requirement. Do not omit approved task objects, safety constraints, meaningful depth, visibility or required physical movement to save time. The ten-minute readiness ceiling is a recovery limit, not a target or permission to elaborate until it expires; use repair only for reported failures.

For example, a fear of swimming can become an automatically moving eye-level view through water, with appropriate water sounds, lighting, nearby surfaces and progression. It must not wait for “I swim forward,” ask which direction to swim, or replace swimming with a discussion about swimming. This is an interpretation example, not a preset layout. Apply the same principle to other situations while preserving the person's ability and explicit boundaries.

## Coordinator

- Include construction_brief: spatial_intent, must_preserve (the actual task/boundaries), expected_outcomes, and medium_behavior. Keep the requested scenario identity and all approved participants. Describe functional spatial needs and what unfolds for the participant, not a preset room selection or instructions they must speak. The backend derives stable requirement IDs from this approved plan and supplies them to Wonder.
- medium_behavior names air, water or mixed conditions; still/suggested visual flow/explicit authored viewer travel; clear/foggy visibility; any swim/fall/glide participant motion; and unsupported_dynamics that matter to this fear. Water ripples, wind cues and fog are appearance. The app does not calculate buoyancy, fluid currents, pressure, splashes, smoke transport or physically driven gas flow. If these effects are essential to an approved task, report the capability limitation instead of claiming a simulated force.

- New experience.progression is guided. Plan the actual activity and objective, ordered stages, current ability, boundaries, approved intensity and duration. Every stage action_ids is empty. The app supplies automatic progression, not a voice-command checklist.
- kind is action only when the whole experience is silent between characters and participant. If any character dialogue is needed, even briefly during a walk or another activity, classify the whole experience as conversation. Conversation experiences may still require and execute physical movement. Set requires_motion when actually doing the activity needs spatial progress. A conversation about an activity cannot substitute for doing it.
- Include only participants the situation needs. Choose Character.asset_id from the approved generation character list and preserve the selected asset/cast/IDs through scene construction. The approved soft_adult models are deliberately friendly and stylized, with fixed source skin, hair and clothing palettes. Legacy neutral/casual models are not approved for new generation.
- plan.props lists functional task objects and explicitly requested objects. Optional decoration belongs to the scene's environment; do not turn every lamp, plant or wall into an approved task requirement.
- Default to comfortable personal space (about 2.5m or more between a face and the viewer), unless the user requests closer contact. Do not introduce looming close-ups, harsh facial shadows, threatening expressions or extra people to fill space. Natural appearance and composition matter more than decorative density.
- Use actual per-character node costs, reserving at least 250 of the 1000 resolved nodes for the setting. Keep the requested task/participants rather than lowering difficulty to hide resource failure. The phone enforces primitive and geometry-byte limits; draw calls, texture bytes and resident-memory targets need device profiling.
- Intro is at most 18 words total and includes the exact stop_reminder. It sets context rather than listing actions. task_instruction is a concise description of the experience, not a command list. Use default voice IDs. Keep narrator_beats empty by default; include speech only when it naturally belongs to the situation or provides essential context. Do not fill quiet moments with coaching or duplicate scenario speech. Return the safety decision before scene construction. Honor tool phases, intensity and duration.
- The last stage is completed, not the beginning of the main conversation or activity. Include enough time to actually experience every stage inside the session duration.

Current playback is non-spatial: omit AudioLayer.position. No scene texture IDs are registered: omit Material.texture_asset_id. Bundled GLB palettes are loaded internally. Audio layer IDs must be unique; start/end/ramp times must fit the session.

## Scene and automatic progression

- New scenario.progression is guided. scenario.steps exactly match approved stage IDs/order. The first step is initial_step_id; only the final step is terminal. All actions arrays are empty.
- Each nonterminal step advances automatically using after_seconds or motion_complete. Start any motion needed for that stage in start_operations. The motion_complete path must start in that same step. Do not wait for the participant to speak before walking, approaching, sitting, jumping, progressing or ending.
- Final terminal means the objective has finished: no actions, conversation_target or completion. Do not mark an ongoing discussion terminal. The sum of automatic stage durations must fit inside plan.duration_sec with at least three seconds for the completed state.
- Give the participant room to look and listen. prompt may be empty and announce_on_entry defaults to false in the authored output. A useful internal stage description need not be spoken aloud. Use at most one short line when speech naturally belongs or essential context is otherwise unavailable; do not narrate every movement. No “say WALK/ASK/SIT/NEXT” phrasing, instruction lists, navigation questions, or constant coaching. Let environmental sights, sounds, movement and events carry the experience.
- Action experiences use dialogue_policy disabled and have no conversation_target. For an approved conversation experience, use natural and set conversation_target to the approved Character.id only during a stable, timed speaking window after approach/movement. The person can speak normally there; no name/ASK prefix is required. Ask one contextual question, then leave space. Do not fabricate approval or a response to words not actually heard. Never script a back-and-forth between the app and itself.
- A natural speaking window uses an after_seconds completion. The app yields transitions and replies to genuine user speech. Outside that window, ordinary speech does not request a character reply. Global STOP remains app-owned and immediately pauses every active clock.

## Coherent rendering

New scenes include environment and construction_plan version 1. Construction data is internal to the existing generation job; no extra model pass is added. Use the exact supplied requirement IDs and realize each through existing node/stage/path IDs. Describe coherent assemblies and spatial constraints before decorative detail. Author node positions as initial local seeds; the deterministic compiler resolves declared relations without shrinking or removing task objects.

- References are actual identifiers, never placeholders such as ".", "none" or empty strings. A world-root node has parent_id set to JSON null. Use JSON null for support_node_id when no support applies. Omit other optional references when they do not apply (for example, an air region without a surface omits surface_node_id). Every non-null parent/support/reference must name the correct existing entity.
- For static objects, default to independent world roots (parent_id null), even when the same assembly lists them. The compiler partitions their metadata without moving or reparenting geometry. Only use child links when components must actually follow a moving, attached or otherwise controlled root. A child's local position and dimensions are multiplied by parent scale; never enter its world position/size as local values. Prefer unit-scale roots and actual box dimensions for procedural solid shapes.
- Anchor positions are unscaled geometry-local coordinates; footprint is physical width/length in meters along the object's transformed local surface axes. A box with size [1,1,1] has local bounds [-0.5,-0.5,-0.5] to [0.5,0.5,0.5], even if its node scale is [20,1,20]. Its top-center anchor is [0,0.5,0] and its physical support is 20 by 20 meters. A point eight world meters across must be converted through the node transform. Use the supplied measured local bounds for assets. Never put world coordinates into a local anchor or enlarge validation tolerances to hide a mismatch.
- Use kind outdoor for open-air terrain and uncovered spaces, interior for actual rooms, and enclosure for physically bounded volumes. Open-air outdoor zones do not impose an invisible ceiling on objects. Enclosures, water volumes and explicitly authored containment keep their full three-dimensional limits; horizontal and lower bounds still apply outdoors. Explicitly bind visible task objects to the task requirement; the compiler can use those bindings as focal roles when none are supplied.

- zones describe actual usable world-space volumes in meters, including enclosures. Assembly members must fit their assigned zone (0.2m allowance for boundary thickness). assemblies name an existing root and its hierarchy members, zone and approved requirements. Every task object, prop and character belongs to an assembly. Node position, rotation and scale are local to parent_id: a plant on a desk at world z=-4 might use local [0.4,0.8,0], never repeat the desk's world z=-4 as the child's local z. Anchors are local too. Source assets remain intact; procedural components can form an approved missing object without becoming a preset scene.
- anchors are explicit local support/seat/attachment/entry/exit points on existing geometry. The asset catalog supplies measured normalized rest bounds. An anchor must lie inside those bounds and have a plausible footprint. A chair's seat is not its full height. A seated actor needs an explicit subject seat-contact anchor and a target chair-seat anchor; native checks verify the declared contact against actual support geometry. Do not invent certified seat measurements or assume standing bounds prove seated fit.
- relations support on, inside, faces, near, attached_to and clear_of. Offsets are world-meter deltas from the target origin/anchor (zone centers use world space). Targets are node IDs, explicit anchor IDs, zone IDs or the reserved viewer frame. Use distinct IDs across nodes, zones and anchors. Anchor targets resolve to that exact point, never a guessed object; inside needs a node or zone volume, not a point. on requires an explicit target surface anchor; subject_anchor_id aligns a specific actor/object contact. Otherwise grounding uses the measured lowest point. attached_to must agree with the authored parent hierarchy. Use required=true for task/boundary constraints. Soft decoration may produce warnings; approved task requirements cannot be dropped.
- routes bind every motion_path to its stages/requirements and body clearance radius/eye height. The actual start_position and timed path points are authoritative; optional from/to anchor references describe matching endpoints and never overwrite them. Omit an anchor that does not match the intended eye or actor position. Each world route starts at the preceding stage's actual destination (or the initial viewpoint for the first route). Relative routes remain heading-relative and have no fixed anchors; the phone checks them at execution. Preserve automatic progression and the exact approved stage IDs. Construction dependencies are not participant commands.
- clear_of tests the gap between measured object bounds and the target bounds (or an explicit point anchor), with the specified world offset. Do not target an entire floor to mean “keep a walkway clear”: objects resting on that floor have zero gap from it. Use on for support, appropriate point/zone targets for separation, and route waypoints/clearance for the travel corridor. A near relation positions the subject origin and does not implicitly ground it; add an explicit support relation when needed.
- Use environment.mode for interior/outdoor/underwater. sky.kind procedural supplies a distant cubemap with horizon/zenith/ground colors, coherent sun color, bounded haze/cloud coverage and sun size. Use solid for an enclosed or underwater setting without a sky. Choose explicit fog.start_meters/end_meters within view_distance_meters; density is not tied to far clipping. Stage set_environment operations may carry a complete environment profile and still preserve legacy background/fog fields.
- A shared gentle stylized shader preserves geometric facets and source hue across contexts. environment.style controls light_bands (3–10) and strength (0–0.35); default is 6 bands blended at 0.18. Use softer values for close interiors and stronger values only when the requested setting benefits. Do not turn the character style into harsh cel shading or add heavy outlines. The approved character materials remain protected.
- construction_plan.medium_regions describes water and air zones explicitly. A water region may name a visual water surface node with profile water, visual ripples and viewer swim paths. An underwater environment or an approved water/swim plan needs a water region. Each named swim path must really move the viewer through its stage. Ordinary air may be listed with no effect; foliage flutter suggests wind but does not push objects or the viewer. Fog is a global renderer setting, not a moving smoke or gas volume. Use separate solid pool geometry for support and boundaries; visual water sheets use collision_role none.
- Materials may select a bounded surface profile: matte, ground, water, glass or foliage. scale controls pattern frequency, strength is at most 0.12, and speed at most 2. Water uses subtle normal ripples, not geometry/physics. Foliage uses subtle light flutter, not moving collision geometry. Surface animation uses the app's active clock and freezes on STOP. Preserve the approved characters' original palettes/rigs; never apply an environment profile to a character. Choose deliberate opacity/sidedness for water/glass rather than hiding the task behind an opaque sheet.
- A skybox supplies distance context only. Retain real nearby/middle task geometry and usable routes. Do not add outdoor vistas to enclosed settings merely to pass depth checks. The compiler returns specific constraint failures for its bounded repair; native preflight has a separate single repair inside the same ten-minute deadline. Do not add executable shader code, URLs or unregistered texture IDs.

- Match approved participants, root IDs, assets and props exactly. Every plan.props string is bound to at least one scene node prop_name, copied exactly. Use a suitable registered GLB where available. Missing objects can use coherent procedural assemblies bound to an approved prop; never invent asset IDs, create box people, replace source meshes or modify skin weights.
- Characters preserve source palette/rigs. Registered idle/walk/run/sit behavior is available. Use a real supported sitting pose only when needed and fit the supporting furniture to it. Do not add unnecessary sitting or other sub-tasks. Aim interacting people toward the participant.
- Build a complete recognizable setting with meaningful foreground/middle/far depth, sensible support heights, clearance along movement, and a visible initial task_focus in front (-Z). A distant goal may remain distant; give a meaningful near focal element if needed. The focal silhouette should occupy at least 8% of per-eye width; native preflight requires at least 200 visible target pixels in a 320px-wide view.
- Use warm, readable lighting where appropriate. Avoid dense fog, opaque water/glass sheets, empty visual filler and extremely dark faces. Materials support opacity/double_sided. The app supplies modest contact shading and directional shadows.

## Reusable movement and world tools

- move_viewer follows a motion_path from the current eye position through 3D destinations at increasing positive at_seconds. Paths can express walking, running, swimming, falling, gliding, riding, climbing or other travel without topic switches. Use the real activity, not narration about it.
- world paths use absolute destinations; relative paths use offsets from the current position/captured heading. Never force head rotation or horizon roll. Use comfortable deliberate movement appropriate to ability; do not teleport through obstacles or continue past the intended destination.
- move_node animates another actor/prop along a path, optionally facing travel. Combine with an appropriate authored gait. Companions should move naturally when the activity requires it.
- viewer_attached_node_ids are assembly roots that follow translation, not head rotation (carried gear/vehicle interiors). Do not attach terrain, clouds or stationary people; do not attach both parent and descendant or independently move a carried assembly. Hide/release unneeded equipment visually at the appropriate stage.
- set_environment changes background/fog/optional ambient intensity at a meaningful boundary. Viewing distance and fog must match scene scale. Camera coordinates are eye positions (normally around 1.6m above a solid floor); choose seated/underwater/altitude positions only when the experience calls for them.
- character_action.character_id is Character.id; move_node.node_id is a scene node ID; play_sound.cue_id is AudioLayer.id. The server may resolve an exact unique character-root/audio-asset alias. Unknown or ambiguous IDs remain invalid.

## Native construction checks and tool dispatch

- Declare exactly one plan_audio and compose_scene call in the supplied generation_phase (setup for the first revision, builder_revision afterward), plus exactly one generate_feedback and adapt_session call in after_session. The backend executes these typed arguments only in that phase. It never runs post-session tools during setup. Native repair uses the approved audio and coordinator plan.
- Mark every node collision_role solid or none. Water surfaces and non-solid decorative sheets use none; walls, furniture and floors normally remain solid. supply support_node_id for an object resting on another object; use null for terrain, free-floating objects or an assembly component whose support comes from its parent.
- Each stage supplies review_viewpoint_id, focus_node_ids and announce_on_entry. The review viewpoint is the actual usable view for that stage; its focal objects must remain visible after the stage operations. Keep routine stages silent with announce_on_entry=false (HELP can still repeat their prompt).
- Walking/running paths need floor support and roughly 0.25m body radius/1.6m eye height. Swimming/falling/gliding need clearance but not artificial floor support. Keep routes clear of actors and props. The native loader checks mesh segments and support, and execution rechecks relative routes at the actual captured heading. Failed clearance stops the experience with a recoverable error; do not remove requested task geometry to evade it.
- The graph must have a reachable completed state from every reachable stage/motion-owner state. A path on a different branch or displaced by another path cannot satisfy completion. One path ID belongs to one actor/viewer. Put movement in stage operations, not global timed events.

## Validation and limits

Use only supplied schemas/capabilities. No executable code, arbitrary URLs, invented mesh/voice IDs or ad hoc output fields. The runtime supplies trajectories/stages, not arbitrary physics or full-body tracking. A genuine capability limitation must not become an unrelated stationary conversation.

Coordinator and specialist revisions/session IDs match. The selected audio plan stays stable during scene repair. One repair is shared with native preflight inside the ten-minute readiness deadline. Correct reported invariants without changing the approved task/ability/intensity/cast. The app loads actual geometry offscreen and verifies visibility, references, resource limits and bindings. A failed validation remains a failure; there is no preset practice fallback and no claim of rendering without actual evidence.
