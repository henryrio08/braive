## Exact prompt set

Each option used this common prefix followed by its option text:

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: original finished app icon for Braive, a voice-first immersive practice app for curious people building confidence through experience. Square, full-bleed opaque very dark evergreen background; warm peach/sand symbol; flat clean vector-like artwork with generous 20% margins, no rounded outer icon mask, no shadows, no gradients, no lettering besides the requested letter, no mockup, no extra words, no medical/brain/goggle symbols. The letter MUST be immediately readable as b or B, never h, n, p, or an abstract arch.

A:

> A friendly, confident lowercase b: tall vertical left stem, one fully closed round bowl on its lower right, and one clearly enclosed circular counter. The counter subtly suggests a sun rising above a tiny path inside the b, while retaining an unmistakable typographic lowercase b silhouette. Solid warm peach, bold rounded geometry, elegantly simple.

B:

> An unmistakable uppercase B with a strong straight left spine and TWO distinct closed rounded bowls, one above the other. A subtle narrow diagonal path runs inside the two counters, suggesting forward movement without breaking the exterior B silhouette. Compact, sophisticated editorial brand mark, solid warm peach.

C:

> An unmistakable bold lowercase b, designed as a smooth continuous rounded band: tall left ascender, fully closed lower-right rounded bowl. Inside the enclosed counter, a small separate forward-pointing chevron in peach gives a feeling of taking the next step. Very clear letterform at tiny sizes; simple modern human and welcoming, not tech-gadget branding.
